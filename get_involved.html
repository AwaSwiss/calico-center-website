Volunteer With Us

Give your time, change lives.

Our volunteers are the heart of our shelter. Whether you have an hour a week or a few afternoons a month, there's a way to help that fit your schedule.

Ways to volunteer include:

  • Socializing cats and kittens
  • Helping at adoption events
  • Assisting with cleaning, feeding and enrichment
  • Taking photos or writing bios to help cats get adopted
  • Administrative or behind the scenes support

No prior experience is required. Just patience, kindness and a love for cats. Training is provided and you'll be making a real difference from day one.

Donate

Your generosity saves lives.

Every donation helps us provide food, medical care, shelter, and love to cats in need. Your support goes directly toward giving cats a second chance at the happy life they deserve.

Your donation supports:

  • Spay and neuter services
  • Vaccinations and emergency medical care
  • Food, litter and enrichment supplies
  • Special care for senior cats and kittens

Whether it be a one-time gift or monthly support, no amount is too small to make an impact.

Foster a Cat

Open your home to help a cat.

Fostering saves lives by giving cats a safe, loving place to stay while they wait for adoption. Whether it's a shy adult cat or a litter of playful kittens, fostering helps them grow, heal and thrive.

We provide:

  • Food, litter, and medical care
  • Support and guidance every step of the way
  • Flexible foster opportunities short term or long term

Fostering is temporary but the impact lasts forever.
