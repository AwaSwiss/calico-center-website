About Us

Calico Center is dedicated to helping cats find safe and loving homes. Our team of staff and volunteers works every day to create the best loving environment for cats to find a home. We rescue abandoned, stray and surrendered cats and connect them with families ready to welcome them.

We believe every cat deserves warmth, care and a safe home.

Whether you're here to adopt, volunteer or support our mission, you're helping us create second chances.

How Adoption Works

Four simple steps to find the right match for you.

1. Browse

View cats availalble for adoption.

2. Apply

Fill out an application.

3. Meet

Schedule a meet and greet with your future pet.

4. Adoption

Complete the adoption and bring your new family member home.
