Adoption Process

Four simple steps to find the right match for you.

Most adoptions are completed within 3-7 days from when you submit your application.

1. Browse

View cats availalble for adoption.

2. Apply

Fill out an application and we will get back to you.

3. Meet

Schedule a meet and greet with your future pet at our shelter.

4. Adoption

Complete the adoption and bring your new family member home.

Requirements

  • You must be 18 years or older to adopt.
  • You must bring a valid government issued photo ID with date of birth.
  • You must bring proof of current address to the shelter.
  • You will need a pet carrier to transport your adopted cat home. You may bring your own or at the time of adoption, you may rent or purchase one from Calico Center.

Additional requirements may apply depending on your cat's needs.

Why Fees Matter

Adoption fees help us continue rescuing and caring for cats in need. Furthermore, adoption fees help cover:

  • spay/neuter surgery
  • a health examination
  • age-appropriate vaccinations
  • medication as necessary
  • microchip implant and registration

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your cats healthy?
All of our cats receive routine veterinary care and are evaluated before adoption. To help you make an informed decision, we share what we know about a cat's health and personality.
Can I adopt if I've never had a cat before?
Absolutely, our team is happy to offer guidance on food, litter, enrichment and helping your new cat adjust.
How long does the adoption process take?
The process usually takes a few days to a week depending on application review and scheduling. Our goal is to ensure the best match for both you and the cat.
What if the adoption doesn't work out?
If things don't go as planned, please contact us. We're committed to the well being of our cats and will work with you to find the best solution, including returning the cat if that is necessary.
What if I already have pets?
That's fine. Let us know about your current pets, and we'll help you find a cat that may be a good fit. Our workers and volunteers will share tips for safe introductions.
